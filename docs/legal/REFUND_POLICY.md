# Refund & Cancellation Policy for PathWise & StudyOS

**Effective Date:** September 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 26, 2026  

At PathWise, we strive to deliver an exceptional educational companion and study operating system for college students. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the procedures and conditions for cancelling subscriptions and requesting refunds for paid services processed via **Razorpay**.

---

## 1. How to Cancel Your Subscription

You maintain full control over your subscription and may cancel recurring billing at any time directly through the app without contacting customer support.

### In-App Cancellation Steps:
1. Open the **PathWise** or **StudyOS** application on your device.
2. Navigate to your **Profile** screen.
3. Tap on **"Manage Subscription"** (or **"Subscription Details"**).
4. Tap the **"Cancel Subscription"** button and confirm your choice.

### What Happens When You Cancel:
- **No Further Charges:** Your subscription will not renew at the next billing cycle, and no further automatic charges will occur.
- **Retained Pro Access:** You will continue to have full access to all Pro features and benefits until the end of your currently paid billing period.
- **Automatic Free Downgrade:** At the end of the billing cycle, your account seamlessly transitions to the Free Tier. All your personal roadmaps, study notes, attendance tracking data, and chat history remain saved.

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility Window

We offer a **7-Day Initial Satisfaction Guarantee** for first-time subscription purchases:

### A. Eligible for Refund
- **First-Time Purchases:** If you purchased a PathWise / StudyOS subscription for the first time and are dissatisfied with the service, you may request a 100% refund within **seven (7) calendar days** of the original purchase date.
- **Technical Failures & Duplicate Deductions:** If you were charged multiple times due to a gateway network issue, or if a payment was deducted by Razorpay but the Pro tier was not activated on your account, you are entitled to a full refund regardless of the timeframe.

### B. Fair Usage Threshold
- To prevent abuse of artificial intelligence API quotas, accounts that have utilized excessive AI model tokens or processed an abnormally high volume of queries (greater than 80% of monthly quota) within the 7-day period may be ineligible for a full refund or may receive a pro-rated refund at our sole discretion.

### C. Ineligible for Refund
- Refund requests submitted after the initial 7-day window.
- Recurring automatic renewals where cancellation was not requested prior to the renewal date (a renewal reminder notification is dispatched prior to billing).
- Token purchases, daily rewards, or virtual items redeemed through in-app reward activities.
- Accounts terminated or suspended due to a violation of our Terms of Service (such as fraudulent use, abuse of CR privileges, or automated scraping).

---

## 3. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request:

1. Send an email to our dedicated billing desk at **`support@pathwise.in`** (or **`billing@pathwise.in`**).
2. Use the subject line: *"Refund Request - [Your Registered Email Address]"*.
3. Include the following details in your message:
   - Your registered PathWise email address.
   - Razorpay Payment ID or Order ID (found on your payment receipt email from Razorpay).
   - The date and amount of the transaction.
   - A brief note explaining the reason for your refund request (helps us improve our service).

Our billing operations team will review your request and verify transaction logs within **24 to 48 hours**.

---

## 4. Refund Processing Time

Once your refund request is verified and approved:

- The refund is initiated immediately through the **Razorpay Payment Gateway API**.
- Funds are returned directly to the **original payment method** used at checkout:
  - **UPI Payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm):** Typically credited within **24 to 48 hours**.
  - **Debit / Credit Cards & Net Banking:** Processed by your issuing bank within **5 to 7 business days** (excluding banking holidays and weekends).
- A confirmation email with the Razorpay Refund Reference Number (ARN/RRN) will be delivered to your email as soon as the refund is initiated.
